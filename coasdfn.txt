Two things worth knowing about how it is wired

Every control is drawn from a `permissions` object the SERVER computed, by asking the same
assertions its writes run (scope.permits). One rule, not a rule and a copy that drift. The old
frontend held the copy in storedUser.position and the endpoints behind it checked nothing.

The advisor is a picker fed by GET /reference/advisors, which returns exactly the AD
memberships assertAdvisorIsValid will accept. The form cannot offer someone the save will
reject, and cannot name a person who does not exist — which is what 12 of the old system's 30
projects did.

Form limits (5 objectives, 3 problems, 15 activities…) are SERVED, not compiled into the
client: GET /reference/limits reads them from the template extraction. A constant in the
frontend would be one template change away from lying to a student.
