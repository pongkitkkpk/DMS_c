Two defects the testing found, not the writing

- Grouped sections read back interleaved, because project_attendance ordinals restart per
  (variant, attendee_type) and the query ordered by ordinal alone.
- Concurrent numbering needed two mechanisms, not one. Ten simultaneous creates in one
  club-year first deadlocked, then produced duplicate keys, because REPEATABLE READ fixes a
  transaction's snapshot at its first read — before it starts waiting for the lock. Both the
  club-row lock and FOR UPDATE on the aggregate are required. Written up in projectService.js.
